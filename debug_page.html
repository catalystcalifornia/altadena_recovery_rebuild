**Fraud Alert**

All official correspondence is issued solely from [Name]@[Department Name].lacounty.gov and donotreply@lacounty.gov email addresses. Communications from other domains are not affiliated with our organization and should not be treated as an official communication. Please also note that the County will never request funds via wire transfer. If you have concerns regarding the validity of an email or have any questions, please contact the respective department directly.

The County’s online payment processor, WorldPay, has implemented a new online payment process with significant changes. For more information, please consult the updated payment guideor contact epiclahelp@lacounty.gov with any questions or issues.﻿﻿

The County’s new Decision Engine can assist you to identify the appropriate permits, applications, and/or licenses required for your project.  Click "Apply" to get started.

Regional Planning offices are open except for Downtown LA and South Whittier, which are now permanently closed. For more information,
visit locations and hours or schedule virtual appointments to discuss your zoning and planning inquiries.

Public Works offices are open to the public. All in-person customers will be served on a first-come, first-served basis.
You may also schedule virtual appointments with our plan checkers.

*NEW* Need help applying for a permit?
Click here to visit our NEW site for a step-by-step guide to the permitting process.

LA County is partnering with Archistar to launch the new eCheck AI Pilot—a tool that reviews building plans for compliance with zoning rules using artificial intelligence—for properties impacted by the Eaton and Palisades Fires. Please visit LA County Recovers to learn more.

8/14/2025 Update: The unified Permit Application is now live. Note there are updates within the application content. All applicable County Departments will review documents within this unified application.

Starting November 1st, 2025, we will no longer offer the Fire Debris Removal (FDR) Permit Applications directly through the Recovery Website or EPIC-LA.  If you need to apply for the FDR permit or if you have any questions regarding an existing permit, please contact our Fire Debris Removal hotline at (888) 479-7328.

Small businesses serving their communities can now apply for streamlined permit and concierge services.
Please visit LA County Small Biz Permit Express.

You can now apply online for Los Angeles County Business License by clicking the Apply tile below and selecting the appropriate business category on the following page.
Please note that you will be required to create an account using your email address to complete the process.
